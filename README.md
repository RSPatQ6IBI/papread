# 📚 Third Author

Imagine having a brilliant research assistant who reads a paper, spots all the citations, and instantly tracks down and downloads the full PDFs of those references for you. That is **Third Author** — your automated literature-review companion. 🚀

`Third Author` is a Python-based tool designed to streamline academic research. Give it a seed PDF, and it will automatically extract the references, search for them on Google Scholar, and download the source PDFs directly to your machine.

---

## ✨ Features

* 📄 **PDF Parsing:** Automatically extracts text and identifies bibliography/reference sections from your local PDFs.
* 🔍 **Smart Extraction:** Isolates individual reference titles and author metadata.
* 🌐 **Google Scholar Integration:** Searches for matching papers online using advanced scraping or API queries.
* 📥 **Automated Downloading:** Fetches and saves the full-text PDFs of the cited works into an organized directory.
* ⏳ **Rate-Limit Friendly:** Built-in delays to respect academic search engines and prevent IP blocks.
